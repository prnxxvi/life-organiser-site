Life Organiser

A personal app built by one student, for their own use. It keeps tasks, spending and classes in one place on their phone.

What it does

  • Tasks with due dates, grouped by School, Tutoring and Personal
  • Spending tracked in Singapore dollars
  • This week's Google Calendar events, with new events added from the app
  • Bank payment alerts in Gmail turned into expense entries automatically

Who can use it

Only its owner. The app runs on the owner's own computer and is not offered to the public.

Google data

The app reads Gmail (read-only) and reads and creates Google Calendar events for the owner's own account. Details are in the privacy policy.
