Privacy policy

Life Organiser · Last updated 21 October 2026

Life Organiser is a personal app used only by its owner (pranai.live@gmail.com). It is not offered to other people. This page explains what it does with Google user data.

What Google data the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only, gmail.readonly): it reads new emails in the owner's inbox to find payment alerts from the owner's bank and other messages that mention tasks or events.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): it shows the owner's events and creates or moves events when the owner asks it to.

How the data is used

  • Payment alerts become expense entries (merchant, amount, date and time).
  • For other emails, the app stores the sender, subject, a short snippet and a summary. It does not store full email bodies.
  • Calendar events are shown in the app and are not copied into its database.
  • The app never sends, deletes or changes email.

Where the data is kept

Everything is stored in a database on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for advertising. No person other than the owner has access.

AI processing

To summarise an email or suggest a task from it, the app may send the email's sender, subject and text to Anthropic's Claude API. That text is used only to produce the summary for the owner. It is not used to train AI models.

Limited Use

Life Organiser's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing data and access

The owner can delete the app's database at any time, and can revoke the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
